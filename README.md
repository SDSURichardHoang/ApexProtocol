Name of the Game: Apex Protocol

Name of Group Members:
- Richard Hoang
- Isaac Acosta
- Grant Becker
- Chris Nguyen

Description of Game: RPG where you play as a robot trying to get to the home bunker, but you have to face series of obstacles of parkour, mobs, and bosses to try to get your way home
In order to get past some of these mobs/bosses, you must retrieve guns whilst doing the parkour, and defeating them when the times comes
Your objective is not easy cause these mobs and bosses will try to take you out, and once they will lower your healthbar to zero you must restart the whole level like every level-based game, and like every game that includes platforming there will be forms of fall-damage implemented

General goals/tasks for each person:

- Richard Hoang: Creation of Gunsystem, Main Character model and movement, Stamina, and HealthBar Scripts
- Isaac Acosta: Creation of Implementing all the animations for the movement and prefab creation of the Health and Stamina Bar to be full functional during playtest + creation of first gun prefab
- Grant Becker: Lighting/Background work + Implementation of enemies movement, animation, and overall creation of enemy
- Chris Nguyen: Background and level formation, as well as implementations of main menu and game over screens + creation of first level scene
